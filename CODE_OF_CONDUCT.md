# Code of Conduct

This organization adopts the **[Contributor Covenant
v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)** across all of
its projects. The full text is authoritative.

The sections below are a short summary plus reporting information. If anything here
conflicts with the Contributor Covenant text, the Covenant wins.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse,
inclusive, and healthy community.

## In scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces — any repository in this
organization, any official chat or forum, and when representing a project in public.

Until that address is wired up, reports may be sent via GitHub: open a private
vulnerability report from the **Security** tab of the repository in question
(`Security` → `Report a vulnerability`). GitHub's private-reporting channel works as a
back-channel for conduct reports too while the dedicated address is pending.

All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. Maintainers are
obligated to respect the privacy and security of the reporter.

## Enforcement

Consequences for violations follow the [Enforcement
Guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines)
of the Contributor Covenant — ranging from a private correction for a first minor
incident, through warnings and temporary restrictions, up to permanent removal from the
community for severe or repeated violations.
